About

My work sits at the boundary of analysis, optimization, and machine learning. I study the energy landscape of t-SNE — why the method can produce misleading or unstable embeddings — along with its large-data limits and the regularity of its solutions, combining rigorous proofs with computational experiments. My advisor is Ryan Murray.

Alongside my thesis I earned a Master's in Financial Mathematics. I am interested in the formulation and solution of optimization problems in finance and data science!
